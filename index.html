The Sunken Corsair · Coral Gardens
Swim toward smaller fish to eat and grow. Avoid larger predators and fishing hooks. Release the left control to stop; swipe the right side to turn, rise or dive.
Choose from 13 detailed fish species. You are one of 8 fish in a rectangular glass aquarium overlooking a softly blurred Chinese restaurant lobby with warm lanterns, carved wood details, and one red neon TAKE OUT sign in the background. Two AI fishers hunt you; seven bot fish compete with you. Left side: drag anywhere to swim (the stick appears under your thumb; direction is relative to the camera). Right side: slide your finger up or down to steer your fish upward or downward, and left or right to turn. Swimming direction stays where you leave it; double-tap the right side to level the fish and center the view. Left side: hold and drag to swim forward, backward, or strafe; releasing the stick stops propulsion. You can use both thumbs together. Explore the Sunken Corsair: enter the broken sides, swim through the cargo hold and captain’s cabin, or descend through the deck hatch. Solid timbers block movement and attacks; shelter inside hides you from fishers. The green target ring tracks edible prey, shrinking as you approach; it is an aim guide only, and a catch requires physical contact. When a chased fish is hidden behind your own fish, its silhouette appears as a thin outline. No targeting ring is drawn on your mouth. Your forward bite area is wider; every fish has expressive eyes and an opening mouth. All fish grow by the same rules: identical prey gives identical base mass gains, with appetite bonuses determined only by species. Size follows a square-root curve. Size-based vibration is supported on compatible devices. Tap the round button for your species-specific ability; when hooked, tap anywhere fast. Puffer inflation temporarily triples combat mass through swallowed water, expands collision volume and lets you swallow smaller fish on contact without adding the water to permanent biomass. Desktop: WASD swim, mouse-drag to steer in all four directions, R/F or ↑/↓ to steer vertically, X to level, ←/→ to turn, space for ability. Every fish eats fish under 90% of its mass. Chase edible fish by steering into them; larger predators are dangerous. Big fish glow to the fishers. Rise fast through the surface to leap out of the water.
