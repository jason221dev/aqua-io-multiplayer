GAME MODE

Explore, eat, grow, and respawn endlessly. Leave whenever you want.

HOW TO PLAY
Swim toward smaller fish to eat and grow. Avoid larger predators and fishing hooks. Hold anywhere on screen to swim forward, steer with the floating thumbstick, and release all touches to stop without changing heading.
Choose from 13 detailed fish species. You are one of 200 competing fish in a rectangular glass aquarium overlooking a softly blurred Chinese restaurant lobby with warm lanterns, carved wood details, and one red neon TAKE OUT sign in the background. Every fish starts at mass 100, seeks floating surface food, and competes to become the biggest predator. Up to two AI fishermen hunt fish. Fish controls: touch and hold anywhere on the playing screen to swim forward. Drag the floating left thumbstick right or left to turn, and up or down to rise or dive. Releasing the last finger stops the fish, preserving its heading and dive angle until you steer again. You can also swipe the right side to steer without needing a second finger. Double-tap the right side to level the fish. You can use both thumbs together. Explore the Sunken Corsair: enter the broken sides, swim through the cargo hold and captain’s cabin, or descend through the deck hatch. Solid hull planks, ribs, deck, masts, sails, rails and spars block swimming and attacks; only intentional openings are passable. Shelter inside hides you from fishers. The green target ring tracks edible prey, shrinking as you approach; it is an aim guide only, and a catch requires physical contact. When a chased fish is hidden behind your own fish, its silhouette appears as a thin outline. No targeting ring is drawn on your mouth. Your forward bite area is wider; every fish has expressive eyes and an opening mouth. All fish grow by the same rules: identical prey gives identical base mass gains, with appetite bonuses determined only by species. Size follows a square-root curve. Size-based vibration is supported on compatible devices. Tap the round button for your species-specific ability; when hooked, tap BREAK LINE once during red tension, or STRUGGLE otherwise. Puffer inflation temporarily triples combat mass through swallowed water, expands collision volume and lets you swallow smaller fish on contact without adding the water to permanent biomass. Desktop: WASD swim, mouse-drag to steer in all four directions, R/F or ↑/↓ to steer vertically, X to level, ←/→ to turn, space for ability. Fish start with 6 seconds of spawn protection. When eaten, you follow the fish that ate you for 6 seconds before respawning (Free Roam), or keep following its predator chain until the match concludes (Last Fish Standing). Unprotected contact follows effective combat mass: heavier eats lighter and ties do nothing. Puffer inflation temporarily multiplies combat mass ×3; temporary ability shields also prevent being eaten. Chase smaller unprotected fish and avoid larger predators. The fisher's fish finder displays the relative size of every fish without making them glow in the aquarium. Fishers cast lifelike 100-mass fish decoys; fish players never see the fishing line. All fish can bite them, but a hook does not award fish growth. Rise fast through the surface to leap out of the water.
